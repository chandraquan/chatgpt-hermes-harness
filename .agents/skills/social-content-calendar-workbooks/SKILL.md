# Social content calendar workbook adaptation

Use the bundled spreadsheet skill. A content calendar is an Excel workbook, not a
Google Calendar event set and not approval evidence.

Inspect authoritative strategy, product facts, identity rules, dates, platforms,
accounts, timezone, cadence, KPIs, and approval owners first. Each requested day
or slot gets one complete row with a unique editorial purpose—never copied filler.

Recommended sheets are `Overview`, `30-Day Plan`, `Production Tracker`, and
`KPI Tracker`. Each row records campaign/content/version, platform, format,
audience, pillar/angle, hook, caption/CTA, visual/product proof, complete image or
first-frame prompt, intended timestamp/timezone, owner, dependencies, approval
gates, status, evidence, and measurement window.

Run row-count/date coverage, uniqueness, formula/reference, platform/account/time
conflict, visual dimension, and internal-link checks. Render or reopen the final
workbook and inspect representative edge rows. Keep `Produced`, `Reviewed`,
`Approved`, `Published/Scheduled`, and `Verified` distinct.
